## Research code with upstream attribution

[TRAM](https://github.com/center-for-threat-informed-defense/tram), from MITRE's Center for Threat-Informed Defense, maps threat-report text to ATT&CK techniques. My [tram repository](https://github.com/MANTHAN137/tram) is a fork of that upstream project. [TTPs-Extractor-from-file](https://github.com/MANTHAN137/TTPs-Extractor-from-file) also carries the upstream TRAM README and attribution.

These links identify the upstream work. A specific original contribution or an original implementation by me is not established by the inspected READMEs, so they are references rather than lead portfolio case studies. Upstream licensing and notices apply.
